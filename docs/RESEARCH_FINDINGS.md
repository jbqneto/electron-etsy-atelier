# Research Findings

## Short market scan

The Etsy seller tooling market is fragmented.

Common categories:

1. SEO and keyword research tools.
2. Product research tools.
3. Listing optimization tools.
4. Print-on-demand publishing tools.
5. Mockup generators.
6. Bulk listing tools.

The opportunity for The Atelier Desktop is to focus on a narrow but painful workflow: local-first production of digital wall art listings.

## Observed competitors and adjacent tools

### eRank

Positioning:

- Etsy SEO;
- keyword research;
- listing optimization;
- shop tracking.

Relevance:

- useful as research inspiration;
- not focused on local image production, mockup templates or final file packaging.

### Alura

Positioning:

- product research;
- Etsy listing optimization;
- listing scoring;
- Chrome extension;
- AI/data-driven Etsy workflow.

Relevance:

- strong for research and listing optimization;
- does not directly solve the local folder/image/mockup/PDF production workflow targeted here.

### EverBee

Positioning:

- product analytics;
- keyword insights;
- market trends;
- Etsy listing/product analysis.

Relevance:

- useful for future research module inspiration;
- not the MVP focus.

### Marmalead

Positioning:

- Etsy keyword research;
- shopper search terms;
- SEO planning.

Relevance:

- useful for future manual/AI keyword helper;
- not focused on production pipeline.

### MyDesigns

Positioning:

- automation platform for POD and digital sellers;
- bulk design;
- AI SEO listings;
- mockups;
- marketplace publishing.

Relevance:

- closest strategic reference;
- likely more cloud/SaaS oriented;
- validates demand for integrated production workflows;
- The Atelier Desktop can differentiate through local-first workflow, reusable custom mockup bases, project folder control and personal production speed.

## Strategic conclusion

The MVP should not compete directly with SEO tools first.

The MVP should win on production workflow:

- project organization;
- local files;
- repeatable mockup generation;
- print ratio export;
- buyer PDF generation;
- final package automation.

Later versions can integrate research, Drive and Etsy publishing.

## Product positioning draft

**The Atelier Desktop is a local-first production studio for Etsy digital wall art sellers who need to move from idea to ready-to-upload listing package without manually managing dozens of folders, mockups, ratios and buyer files.**
